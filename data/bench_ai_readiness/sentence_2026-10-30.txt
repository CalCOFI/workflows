In a pilot of 24 questions about CalCOFI data (retrieval, cross-dataset integration, analysis and unanswerable items) put to off-the-shelf models (Haiku 5.5, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5; 5 model and effort settings, 3 runs each), the models answered 12–31% correctly from training alone, 76–96% when they had to find and download the providers' public files themselves, and 89–96% with the integrated release, which also cut the cost per answer by 34–69% and the median time by 64–79%. The release helped the smaller models most (Haiku 5.5: 76% to 93%); the strongest agents (96% from raw files) matched it and, in doing so, exposed a cruise-key defect in the release, the kind of release-to-release check Aim 2 proposes.
