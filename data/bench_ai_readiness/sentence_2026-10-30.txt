A pilot (calcofi.io/workflows/bench_ai_readiness.html) shows the design works. We put 24 questions to off-the-shelf models (three Claude models at five model and effort settings, three runs each). They answered 12–31% correctly from training alone, 76–96% when they had to find and download the providers' public files themselves, and 89–96% with the current release, which also cut the cost per answer by 34–69% and the median time by 64–79%. The release helped the smallest model most (76% to 93%); the strongest agents matched it from raw files and, in doing so, exposed a cruise-key defect in the release, which is the release-to-release check the benchmark is designed to provide.
