## The ask (Ben, 2026-09-07)

Snazz up the calcofi.io landing page to *quickly, visually* communicate the breadth and vastness in time and
space of what CalCOFI has collected. The Datasets view and the app screenshots exist; wanted is something more
visual that connects **the ship, the species and the instrumentation**, and a layout that is not so linear. The
reference is what he did for MBON — a hero illustration of the methods on the landing page, going to per-method
pages — but with less detail here, linking out to calcofi.org for the methods. calcofi.io stays the data-centric arm
of calcofi.org and should be an exciting place to visit; **News** for datasets, apps and features as they land.
Scientific but stylized, at ease and excited, in brand v2. After seeing the artifact and his own three generations
(Gemini for the hero, ChatGPT for the category spots, Recraft for a ship), he chose the mockup's SVG route.

## Goal

A first screen that *shows* what CalCOFI does and how far the data reach — the ship on Line 90 with its gear and
the life it samples drawn to depth, the station grid and every dataset's years measured from the release, six
numbers, and a handful of ways in — before the catalog, which stays exactly as it is. Every colour a token, every
number a measurement, every hotspot a link to calcofi.org's own method page, and a news log that mostly writes
itself.

## Non-goals

- No generated imagery in cut 1 (Ben's decision); no raster hero. The painted layer, if ever, is cut 2 and sits
  *behind* the SVG overlay.
- No change to the catalog band, the dataset pages, the product cards, `products.yml`'s three-slug contract, the
  header's lockup rule, or any existing brand v2 token, class or rule.
- No new dataset fact typed on the site: the record and the catalog are the only sources; the sampling constants
  are protocol, not data, and say so.
- No framework, no map library, no external asset (fonts stay self-hosted; the artifact's Google Fonts link was
  for the artifact only).
- No `/methods/` pages on calcofi.io — the pins link to calcofi.org (`/sampling-info/methods/…`).

## Design

### D-1 · The hero is the section

Line 90 as an oceanographic section, **west on the left, coast on the right** (the Explorer's Sections rule), in an
inline `<svg viewBox="0 0 1400 660">` built at page load by `assets/section.js` from the brand sprite and a table of
constants (Appendix A):

- **Sky** 0–200 (`--sky0 → --sky1`), **surface** at y = 200 (`--surf`, the cyan), **water** 200–600 (five stops
  `--w0 … --w4`, pale cyan to navy), **sea floor** in `--floor` with a `--floor-line` stroke, a low headland breaking
  the surface at the right edge. **Depth is a broken axis**: 0–500 m linear over 250 px (the euphotic zone and the
  standard cast get the room), 500–4,000 m compressed below a break glyph — labels `0 · 100 … 500 · 1,000 … 4,000`,
  title `depth (m)`; the station axis reads `120 110 100 90 80 70 60 53 45 37 35 30 28` — **taken from
  `grid.geojson` where `line == 90`** (the standard pattern), 12.5 px per station unit, captioned `← offshore ·
  Line 90 · station · 4 nmi per unit · coast →`.
- **The ship** (a navy silhouette, bow to the coast: hull, bridge block, wheelhouse with one window strip, stack,
  mast with a sensor crossbar, stern A-frame, a CTD davit, the observer's binoculars on the flying bridge) at
  x ≈ 600–800 on the surface.
- **The gear, to its true depth** (constants of the protocol, each with a `// source:` comment naming the
  calcofi.org page): the **CTD wire** to **515 m** with **14 bottle dots at the standard depths** `0 10 20 30 50 75
  100 125 150 200 250 300 400 500` and the rosette drawn as a frame with three Niskins; the **bongo** on a **45°**
  wire to **210 m** (two rings, two cones); the **PairoVET** vertical to **70 m**; the **manta** on the surface;
  the **CUFES** intake at **3 m** as a dotted line trailing astern; the **METS** crossbar on the mast; two dashed
  sight lines from the binoculars to a bird and a whale.
- **Thirteen pins, one per `metadata/category.csv` row**, each a circle with the category's sprite glyph, at the
  depth that kind of measurement lives (Appendix A): physical at the rosette, chlorophyll / DIC / nutrients on the
  wire at 10 / 100 / 150 m, eDNA at 20 m **drawn `held`** (dashed), meteorology at the mast, phytoplankton 0–50 m,
  picoplankton 0–100 m, zooplankton 0–210 m, krill at 300 m with a dashed vertical-migration arrow, fish eggs &
  larvae near the surface, mesopelagic fish near 1,000 m, marine mammals at the surface, seabirds in the sky. A
  pin is an `<a>` to the category's calcofi.org method page (`href` in Appendix A), with a `<title>` of the form
  `Zooplankton — 5 datasets in the release, 1 held · methods on calcofi.org` **whose counts come from the record**
  (the plugin hands `site.data.catalog` the per-category counts already). Labels are one to three words in
  `--lab` with a `--halo` paint-order stroke; below 420 px of drawing they switch to `--lab-d` on `--halo-d`.
- **Text**: in the mockup the eyebrow, `FROM SHIP TO SCREEN`, the lede and the CTA are SVG `<text>`; **in the
  real page they are HTML** (`.hero-copy`, absolutely positioned over the sky at ≥ 900 px, stacked above the
  drawing below that) so they wrap, size with `clamp()`, and stay selectable and translatable. The lede is one
  short line — **`Temperature to whales, surface to the deep, since 1949 — open and integrated.`** (Ben's choice,
  2026-09-07: the two ends of the pin list name environment and biology, "surface to the deep" is the depth claim
  without a number some cast would contradict, and "cruise after cruise" was dropped because the drawing carries
  the repetition) — 77 characters, so it ends before the ship's wheelhouse at 17 px with the ship where it is. **The one yellow CTA stays here** (`Explore the data` → `/explore/`); the two text links
  (`Browse the datasets`, `Build on it`) follow.
- **Motion**: the rosette translates down the wire and back on a 26 s loop; the CUFES dots flow. Nothing else. Both
  off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No JS is needed for theme: the drawing is tokens.
- **Responsive**: `.sec` is `width:100%; height:auto`. At < 900 px the copy stacks above; at < 700 px the labels
  (`.lab`) and gear captions hide (pins and `<title>`s stay); at < 500 px the axes' tick labels hide. Never a
  horizontal scroll.

### D-3 · The reach — where and when, measured

- **The map** (`assets/reach.js`, `<svg viewBox="0 0 520 640">`): equirectangular over lon −134…−105, lat 19…49
  (x = (lon+134)/29·520, y = (49−lat)/30·640, which is cos 33°); `land.geojson` as `--map-land` paths; the standard
  and extended lines as thin polylines (`.trk`, `.trk.e` dashed); stations by pattern — historical `--cc-stone` r 2,
  extended `--map-mark` hollow r 2.6, standard `--map-mark` filled r 3; a **cruise sweep**: the standard stations
  pulse in line order 93.3 → 90 → 86.7 → 83.3 → 80 → 76.7, alternating direction per line, over a 12 s loop, with a
  cyan ripple ring; a **year odometer** 1949 → 2026 over the same 12 s in the corner (static `1949 – 2026` under
  reduced motion). Place labels: San Diego, Pt. Conception, San Francisco, Cabo San Lucas, Columbia River; line
  labels at the west ends of 93.3, 76.7 and 60. Caption: *218 grid cells · 67 standard (quarterly since 1985) · 37
  extended (winter, spring) · 114 historical — the 1950s pattern ran from 48°N to 20°N.* A station click opens
  `app.calcofi.io/station/` (cut 3 passes the cell).
- **The years strip** (`<svg viewBox="0 0 1000 296">`): one row per dataset in `datasets[]`, sorted by
  `coverage.year_min` then name; one cell per year 1949–2026, `fill` = the dataset's `color`, `fill-opacity`
  `0.3 + 0.7·√(n_roots / max_roots_in_row)`; a `<title>` per cell (`name · year · n casts or tows · n
  observations`); decade rules and labels; a top bar per year counting datasets with data; the release version as a
  rule at the right edge. **A dataset with no `coverage.years` is drawn as one hatched bar** from its `temporal`
  string (`phytoplankton`, region-pooled; `pic-zooplankton`, samples only), `fill-opacity .22`, dashed stroke, with
  a `<title>` saying *asserted, not measured*. Gaps are shown, never smoothed.
- **The data reach the browser as one inline JSON**: the plugin builds `site.data.reach` at build time — the 218
  centroids `{k, l, s, p, x, y}` (2-decimal), the land rings (2-decimal), and per dataset `{name, color, temporal,
  ymin, ymax, years: [[year, n_roots, n_obs] …]}` — and `index.html` writes it once as
  `<script type="application/json" id="reach">{{ site.data.reach | jsonify }}</script>` (≈ 40 KB). No new HTTP
  request, no `grid.geojson` in the browser (407 KB), and the record stays the only source.

### D-4 · The bento — ways in, then the catalog

A 6-column grid (`grid-auto-rows: 150px; gap: 16px`) between the numbers band and `#datasets`, then the catalog
band **unchanged**, then the product sections as today (bands) — or as a tab row (Decision 3):

| tile | span | content | link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where | 2 × 3 | the map + odometer | Station Explorer |
| When | 4 × 2 | the years strip | the Explorer (`?datasets=`) |
| Latest release | 2 × 1 | version · date · tables · rows · DOI | schema · query · notes · cite (the release strip's links) |
| Ship's log | 2 × 2 | the four newest entries | `/news/` · feed |
| Explore | 2 × 2 | `images/explore_{light,dark}.png` + the five lens glyphs | `/explore/` |
| Get the data | 2 × 2 | tabs R · Python · SQL · ERDDAP · Parquet with one snippet each (R first) | calcofi4r · calcofi4py · db-query |
| Life | 2 × 1 | `2,614 taxa` + six organism glyphs | the Explorer's Browse |

Tiles are `.tile` (border, `--radius-card`, `--panel-2`), content sets their height, and **no tile ends in a
button**: the one CTA is in the hero (brand rule), every tile ends in an uppercase text link. At 860 px the grid
goes to two columns; at 375 px to one. The header gains **News** (Decision 2). The mockup's browser-frame scaler
(`.stage`, `.page`) is mock-only and is not ported.

### D-5 · The ship's log

`/news/`, a tile, and `feed.xml`, from four sources merged and date-sorted by `_plugins/news.rb`:

| type | source | generated |
|---|---|---|
| release | `_data/versions.json` (+ each version's `RELEASE_NOTES.md` first heading, fetched by `fetch_release.sh`, or the version alone) | yes |
| dataset | `datasets[].since_version` → *X enters the release* on that version's date | yes |
| app | `products.yml` `added: YYYY-MM-DD` (new key; back-fill the 2026 ones from git) → *New: title* | yes |
| data · site · paper | `_data/news.yml` rows `{date, type, title, body, url}` (hand-written) | no |

The tile shows four, `/news/` all with a type filter (the mockup's chips, plain JS), `feed.xml` via `jekyll-feed`
so calcofi.org's WordPress can pull it into *News & Updates*; program news (cruises, people, papers) stays on
calcofi.org and is linked. **Type chips are quiet** — `.cc-chip-quiet` or `--accent-bg`; **never `--warn`** (the
UI refresh's rule: yellow marks a state that needs attention, never a kind of thing). *The mockup's release chip
used `--warn-bg`; that is a correction to make, not a look to copy.* A product with `added:` within 30 days wears a
small `new` dot on its card and the header's News link.

### D-6 · Below the bento, nothing moves

`#datasets` with its three includes, the masonry, the filter row, the reference band; the product sections with
their groups, lens key, featured grid and cards; the footer. The sticky `.section-tabs` row is kept and moves to
just above `#datasets` (it is the catalog's sub-nav; on the first screen it would sit over the drawing), gaining

### D-7 · Theme, motion, accessibility

Every colour in the drawing, the map and the strip is a `--token` (the brand set plus the additive
`--sky*`, `--w*`, `--floor*`, `--ship`, `--wire`, `--surf`, `--sun`, `--pin-*`, `--lab*`, `--halo*` — added to
`brand/v2/theme.css` **as new names only**, both themes, with README rows and specimen swatches, Decision 5); so
`?theme=dark` and the toggle restyle everything with no JS and `cc:theme` needs no listener. The SVGs carry
`role="img"` and an `aria-label`; every pin is a real link with a `<title>`; every cell has a `<title>`; the
odometer is `aria-hidden` with the caption carrying the years; focus rings on pins and chips; the two animations
and the sweep stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`; Lighthouse accessibility stays 100.

### D-8 · Keep it true

`scripts/check_layout.py` gains `/` with assertions that can only pass by the page being right: the hero's SVG is
drawn ≤ 62 vh at 1470 px and the copy does not overlap the ship's bounding box; no horizontal scroll at 375 px;
no `.tile` drawn > 1.25 × its natural height; every pin `href` answers 200 (ranged GET, the way
`build_workflows_index.R` probes); the six numbers on the page equal the record's (the probe reads the inline
`#reach` and `site.data.catalog` values back); the strip has exactly `datasets.length` rows; no element on the
first screen computes to `--warn` except the CTA. `check-brand.yml` and `pr.yml` run it as they run the rest.
`shots.yml pages:` gains `home` (both themes, 1200 × 750) so the luminance check covers the front door and
calcofi.org has a current card to link.

## Decisions (1–10 confirmed by Ben 2026-09-07 — the bold recommendation is the decision)

1. **The drawing route — SVG from the sprite, no generated art in cut 1.** *Confirmed by Ben 2026-09-07.*
2. **News in the header** — a literal link after the generated section links in `_layouts/default.html`
   (`sections:` stays the product-section list; News is a page, not a section) · or a `news` entry in `sections:`
   with `kind: page` that the loop skips for counts. **Recommend the literal link.**
3. Product sections below the catalog — **keep the bands as they are** (zero risk, the cards and groups untouched) ·
   or fold Explore / Access / Build / Students into one tab row with the four grids switched by JS. Recommend the
   bands now; the tab row is a later, separate change if the page still reads long.
4. The sticky tab row — **keep it, moved to just above `#datasets`** · or drop it (the header links cover it).
5. The new drawing tokens — **add to `brand/v2/theme.css` as new names** (specimen swatches + README rows, both
   themes, contrast noted) so the Explorer's Sections lens and ctd-transects can share the water ramp later · or
   keep them local to `style.css`. Recommend the brand, additively.
6. The sea floor — **build `line90_floor.json` from GEBCO in cut 1** (½ hour with `cc_transect_bathy()`; the
   drawing becomes measured) · or ship the mockup's profile and mark it drawn.
7. The `Life` tile's count — **taxa from `catalog.json`** (needs the fetch) · or drop the tile until the record
   carries a taxon count. Recommend the fetch: it is the same `RELEASE_BASE/{version}/` the record comes from.
8. `added:` back-fill dates for the existing products — **from each product's first commit in `products.yml`**
   (`git log -S'key: <key>' --diff-filter=A`), so the log is honest about when the site started carrying them.
9. Motion budget — **the rosette cast, the CUFES flow, the sweep and the odometer, nothing else**; all off under
   reduced motion. (A gentle water shimmer was considered and dropped: it reads as decoration.)
10. The cut-2 painted layer — **not scheduled**; if wanted, Ben's Gemini P1 (Appendix B) is already close to the
    brief and would sit behind the overlay as a `<picture>` with a dark-ground regeneration.

## Risks and what bounds them

- **The hero pushes the catalog below the fold** — that is the point, but the catalog is the site's strongest
  page: bounded by the numbers band's `Browse the datasets` link, the sticky tab row above `#datasets`, and the
  header's Datasets link; measured: the catalog's first tile must start within 2.2 screens at 1470 px.
- **A label collision in the drawing** at some width — bounded by hiding `.lab` under 700 px and by the layout
  check's copy-vs-ship overlap assertion; the mockup's positions were tuned at 1400 units and hold at any width
  because the SVG scales as one unit.
- **`catalog.json` missing for an older promoted release** — bounded by rendering no taxon tile (D-2's rule) and
  by `fetch_release.sh` treating it as optional with a NOTE.
- **A generated news row that is wrong** (a `since_version` on a dataset that predates the catalog) — bounded by
  showing only versions in `versions.json` and by `news.yml` overrides keyed on `dataset_key`.
- **Yellow creeping back** (the release chip in the mockup) — bounded by the D-8 assertion: nothing on the first
  screen computes to `--warn` but the CTA.
- **Brand freeze** — every token is a new name, both themes, with a swatch; nothing existing changes. If a reviewer
  reads a token as changing a look, it goes local to `style.css` instead.
- **The site's own screenshot (`home`) captures mid-animation** — the shot recipe waits 6 s and sets
  `prefers-reduced-motion` via the emulation flag so the card is deterministic.
