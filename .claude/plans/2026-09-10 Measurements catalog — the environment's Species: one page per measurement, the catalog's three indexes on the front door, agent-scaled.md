## Findings

- **F1** The environment half of the catalog has no index. "Which datasets measured nitrate, when, and how do I get it" is
  answered today only by reading five dataset pages or by the Explorer's picker at runtime (not indexable). The species
  catalog's pattern fits one-for-one: one record per key generated at release, one generator, one page per key with
  JSON-LD, one index with search, every fact read.
- **F2** The key already exists. `measurement_type.variable` is the crosswalk the Explorer reads from `coverage.json`
  (five pairs today); a measurement page is keyed by it, else by the canonical `measurement_type` — 79 keys.
- **F3** Two facts the page needs are not data yet: a **label** per key (the registry's `description` is a column note)
  and, for 23 series, a **NERC concept** — which under the exact-match rule means "no concept says exactly this" and is
  what the page must say.
- **F4** The same NERC concept across datasets is not always the same measurement. The bottle's `nitrate` and the CTD
  files' `btl_nitrate` share NTRAZZXX and are plausibly the same bottles (double count if merged); a replicate is not a
  mean; an underway intake is not a cast. P01 identity is necessary evidence for a `variable`, never sufficient; the rule
  needs the sample kind.
- **F5** The catalog surfaces quality facts nobody states today — the 99 °C bottle sentinel with no declared bound, the
  716 undocumented flag codes, the headline CTD series carrying no flag at the release grain. Stated on the page as
  measured and filed as questions, they are worth the catalog on their own.
- **F6** On the front door Species is one tile's link; the header and the section nav do not name the indexes; the band's
  counted things ("1,008 species", "316 M measurements") are dead numbers; the Data section's search only filters the
  dataset grid.
- **F7** Picoplankton's four cell counts are measurements (rows of `obs_env`) under a biology category. They belong in the
  Measurements catalog under their own category heading, said plainly; and their colour needs the name beside it here.
- **F8** The Explorer needs nothing new to be the map behind the page: `?var=` opens prefilled; later it can read the
  labels the registry gains instead of `UNIFIED`.
- **F9** Size is small: 79 keys × 84 series with 78-year maps ≈ 250 KB at build, ~60 KB inlined on the index.

## Decisions (each with a recommendation; the bold one is what the workstreams build)

### D1 — The word

(a) "Variables", the Explorer's word. (b) **"Measurements"** — the door word, Ben's word, and the band's word (316 M
measurements); each page is *a measurement* (Temperature); a dataset's column is a *series* (`measurement_type`); one row
is *a value*; "variable" stays the term of art in the docs, the Explorer and the registry column, exactly as "species"
sits over "taxon". *Recommend (b)*; the glossary gains *measurement · series · value* beside *species*.

### D2 — The key, and what one page holds

**One page per measurement key at `/measurements/{key}/`** — `variable` where set, else the canonical
`measurement_type` (`/measurements/temperature/`, `/measurements/nitrate/`, `/measurements/btl_temperature/`); the key is
already URL-safe. The series are listed under it, one per `measurement_type × dataset`, each with the dataset's own
column and flag column ("what the source called it" — the `dataset_taxon` of this catalog) and a quiet pill where the
series is a *sensor mean*, a *replicate*, a *reported pre-QC* twin, or has *no flag at this grain*. A series that is not
canonical (the `temperature_1/2` sensors, `potential_temperature_*`, the METS `tsg2..5`) gets no page and is listed under
its dataset as `full_resolution_only[]` (the `vocabulary_only[]` of this catalog) and on the page of the key it belongs
to. *Recommend this over one page per series*: the integrated database integrates — Ben's rule for species holds here.

### D3 — The crosswalk rule (which series become one measurement), and the label

**A `variable` is assigned only where (i) the NERC P01 concept is identical, (ii) the units are the same or a declared
conversion, (iii) the series come from the same kind of sample (cast profile with cast profile), and (iv) the two are not
plausibly the same physical samples.** The five pairs the Explorer carries pass; nothing else in v2026.09.06 does
(Appendix B): the CTD files' `btl_*` beside the bottle dataset fail (iv) and stay their own keys, stated on the bottle
measurement's page as *related* with the reason; `alkalinity_rep1` ↔ `alkalinity` and `dic_rep1` ↔ `dic` fail (iii)
(replicate vs mean) and become `proposed` questions for the DIC/bottle providers; underway vs cast fail (iii). **Labels
live in a new registry, `metadata/variable.csv`** (`variable, label, description, units, nerc_p01, category,
is_unified`), one row per key the release carries, read with `read_variable()`, appended with `register_variables()`,
never a bare `write_csv()`; the Explorer's five hard-coded labels seed it. *Recommend this over a `label` column on
`measurement_type.csv`*: a label belongs to the key, not to each of its series, and the Explorer already wants the file.

### D4 — The record: `measurements.json`, written at release beside `taxa.json`

**`calcofi4db::build_measurements_catalog(con, record, registries)` → `measurements.json`** (schema
`inst/schema/measurements.schema.json`, `schema_version` 1.0; the shape is Appendix A): counts, `datasets[]` (the five,
with `full_resolution_only[]`), `measurements[]` one per key with `series[]` carrying per-year, per-month, per-depth-band
and per-flag counts, observed quantiles, the registry's bounds, the source and flag columns, the P01/P06 ids, and
`related[]` (same P01 family or same source quantity, kept apart on purpose, with the reason). Written in
`release_database.qmd` step **3b″** after `taxa.json`, uploaded with the sidecars, in `RELEASE_REQUIRED_OBJECTS`; a
`test_release.qmd` gate (`measurements_catalog`: schema valid; `counts.obs_env_rows` == `obs_env` rows; every
`series[].measurement_type` is in the registry; every `dataset_key` in the record; the sum of `series[].n_values` over
keys == `obs_env` rows). Bridge exactly as `taxa.json` did: `MEASUREMENTS_RELEASE_URL` (a staging record built from
v2026.09.06) until a promoted release carries one; without either, no measurement pages and the doors fall back to the
Explorer's variable picker (the D-2 rule: nothing typed). *Recommend this over extending `coverage.json`*: it is the
Explorer's first-paint payload; the two share `build_coverage()`'s query so the counts cannot disagree.

### D5 — What the index shows

1. **One timeline table** (the mock's centrepiece): one row per measurement grouped by category, one bar per dataset
   from its first to its last year on a 1949 → 2026 axis, then units · datasets (dot + name) · values · depth range;
   hover a bar for the series, years, values, casts, depths, column, flag and P01; search (`?q=`) over names, keys,
   series, datasets, units and P01 ids; category chips. It answers Ben's question — *which datasets measured a given
   measurement over time* — in one figure.
2. **The category × dataset matrix** (cell = number of measurements, one sequential ramp) beside **the datasets** list
   (dot · name · series · years · values) — the legend that keeps identity off colour alone.
3. **No icicle**: a flat vocabulary has no hierarchy worth drawing; the timeline is the figure.

### D6 — What a measurement page holds

Crumb (Measurements › category › key) · eyebrow · the label · one line of what it is · the ids (P01, P06 units, key,
series) · stats (values · casts · years · datasets · depth · seasons) · **Measured in** (one row per series with the
dataset's column, flag and pills) · **the years strip** (the species page's) · **by depth** (eight bands, one thin bar
per dataset) · **by month** · **Range & quality** (declared bounds, observed min/median/max and 5th–95th, the flag
counts by code, `qual_ok`, whether a climatology exists) · **Related measurements** (the same quantity from another
platform or sample, each its own page, with the reason it is kept apart) · **Ways in** (Explorer prefilled on `?var=`,
the anomaly section, db-query with the query in the shell, ERDDAP per series where the probe says the table carries
`measurement_type`, Parquet through the catalog, R, Python) · **This page as data** (`{key}.json`) · **Cite** (the
release; each dataset's citation on its page). JSON-LD: `schema.org/DefinedTerm` in the NERC P01 `DefinedTermSet` with a
`PropertyValue` per series (`unitCode` from P06), validated by `check_jsonld.py`. No map: the Explorer is one click away.

### D7 — The navigation: six words, submenus where a section has tabs, and every crowded section tabbed (Ben, 2026-09-10)

Ben's scheme replaces the first cut's *Data* rename and the extra header links:

```
DATA    datasets · species · measurements     (each with its own landing page)
APPS    explorer · general · dataset · student (Students folds into Apps)
ACCESS  services (erddap · storage · db-query) · packages (calcofi4r · calcofi4py)
BUILD   workflows · calcofi4db · db-schema
DOCS    the book, promoted to the top level — documentation for everyone, not only builders
NEWS
```

**What the front door builds:** (1) the header carries the six words plus *calcofi.org ↗* — seven items, as today; DATA,
APPS and ACCESS get a **submenu** on hover / focus-within (CSS only, keyboard-reachable, closed by Escape) listing that
section's tabs with a count pill, because those are the sections whose sub-items have somewhere to go — the three
catalog pages under DATA, the tabs under APPS and ACCESS; BUILD, DOCS and NEWS have none. On touch a tap on the word goes
to the section. (2) The sticky section bar lists the four on-page sections — DATA · APPS · ACCESS · BUILD — with their
counts (DATA reads *16 datasets · 1,008 species · 79 measurements*). (3) **DATA** is one section with three tabs, each
with a count pill: *Datasets* (today's grid, the release strip and the realm heads carrying their doors), *Species* (the
species index's head — the counts, its search, *Open the species catalog →*) and *Measurements* (the same for
`/measurements/`); one search over the three indexes sits above the tabs. (4) **APPS** is tabbed *Explorer · General ·
Dataset · Student* with pills (1 · 4 · 3 · 9 today; the superseded and archived cards keep their chips inside their
tab); the Students section is retired. (5) **ACCESS** is tabbed *Services · Packages* (3 · 2). (6) **BUILD** keeps three
cards; the docs card leaves it for the DOCS link. (7) DOCS and NEWS are pages, so the header links them and no section is
drawn; the ship's-log tile keeps linking News. (8) The band's *species* and *measurements* tiles link their pages and the
Life tile becomes *What's observed*, as before. `products.yml` grows one notion — `sections[].tabs[]` (id, title, blurb)
and a `tab:` on each product (replacing Explore's `group:`) — and two `kind: page` sections (docs, news) so the header is
still generated from one list; the section ids `datasets`/`explore`/`access`/`build` keep their anchors (titles change),
`students` is removed, and the card keys — the uptime and analytics slugs — do not change. *Recommend all eight*; the
submenus are the one optional piece (open question 8).

### D8 — Picoplankton, and the one colour that clashes

**The four cell counts are listed under *Picoplankton & Bacteria* in the Measurements catalog and nowhere in the species
catalog**, the category header saying so ("a biology category: cell counts, rows of `obs_env`"). A bar is never the only
cue — the dataset's name is in the row and the legend above — and the D12 palette gains one constraint at its next
re-spread: bottle ↔ picoplankton appear together in this catalog. *Recommend this over re-colouring now*: D12 has not yet
reached the record.

### D9 — What the catalog surfaces is fixed at the ingest when it is plainly impossible, and questioned when it is not (revised by Ben, 2026-09-10; DONE the same day)

Ben: the 99 °C "is clearly wrong … NULLify directly in the db, ideally in the db release underway". Measured on
v2026.09.06 before touching anything: it is **one bottle** (2020-07-33P4, station 93.3/30, 50 m — its neighbour at 50 m
reads 56.87 °C, and both bottles' salinities read 9.50 and 21.06 beside 33.6 above and below), not a DBF-era 9-fill —
no bottle series carries a −99 / 999 pattern (the 1,739 temperatures at exactly 9 or 99 are 9.0 °C readings). The same
scan found six potential densities of 216–251 kg/m³ (2014-11-32NM, 2015-04-32NM) and one surface oxygen of −200.2 ml/L /
−8,740.6 µmol/kg / −3,605 % on the 2020 cruise. **Done (2026-09-10, `ingest_calcofi_bottle.qmd` § Declare and Enforce
Physical Bounds):** the bottle ingest declares `temperature` −2…40 °C and `sigma_theta` 15…35 kg/m³ (the CTD's own
bounds) and `oxygen_ml_l` −0.5…15, `oxygen_umol_kg` −20…700, `oxygen_saturation` −1…250 — the oxygen floors just below
zero so the four Winkler zeros of the line 83.3 oxygen minimum stay — and `drop_out_of_bounds()` deletes the eleven rows
before `obs` is emitted, exactly as the CTD and METS ingests do; `RELEASES.md # Unreleased` carries the entry; the
notebook was re-rendered by hand for the release underway. **Not fixed, questioned (Q13, `high`):** salinity — cruise
2021-05-3322 carries 918 values of 24.19–27.06 PSU to 568 m across 39 stations with `S_qual` 254–344, a whole cruise the
bounds skill forbids deleting by a floor; the two corrupt 2020 bottles' salinities ride with it until the provider rules.
The measurement page states bounds as the registry declares them and shows the observed range; a series whose observed
range breaks no declared bound shows no chip; a series with no bound says so (`no_bound`).

## Appendix A — the record, in shape (`measurements.schema.json` 1.0)

```
{ schema_version, release{version, release_date},
  counts{measurements, series, datasets, obs_env_rows, full_rows, pages},
  datasets[]  {dataset_key, dataset_name_short, color, category{key, name, icon, realm},
               n_series, n_values, year_min, year_max,
               full_resolution_only[] {measurement_type, description, table}}          ← no page; listed here
  measurements[] one per key (variable ∥ canonical measurement_type):
              {key, slug, label, description, units, units_nerc_p06, nerc_p01, category{key, name, icon, realm},
               is_unified, derivation, climatology (bool),
               bounds{valid_min, valid_max, declared_by[] measurement_type},
               totals{n_values, n_samples, n_roots, n_datasets, year_min, year_max, depth_min_m, depth_max_m},
               series[] one per measurement_type × dataset:
                 {measurement_type, dataset_key, source_column, qual_column, description, units, nerc_p01, is_canonical,
                  n_values, n_samples, n_roots, n_cells, n_cruises, year_min, year_max, depth_min_m, depth_max_m,
                  years{"1949": n, …}, months[12], depth_bands{"0-10": n, "10-50": n, …, "2000+": n},
                  qual{"none": n, "6": n, …}, qual_ok_n, observed{min, p05, p50, p95, max}, flags[]},
               related[] {key, why}}   ← same P01 family or same source quantity, kept apart on purpose; why is one of
                                         same_bottles · replicate_vs_mean · underway_vs_cast · pre_qc_twin
  flags[] ∈ sensor_mean · replicate · reported_pre_qc · no_bound · sentinel_suspected · no_flag_at_grain · no_p01
}
```

Rules that hold because they hold in the record: a number is read, never typed; `label` comes from `variable.csv` and,
absent a row, from the canonical series' `description` with a `no_label` flag (never invented by the builder); a key with
no P01 says so; the record is deterministic (no wall clock, no network); the order is the category order then `n_values`.
