## The prompt

You are polishing a 21-slide, 16:9 PowerPoint deck: Ben Best's part of Mark Gold's keynote at the CalOOS summit on
2026-10-13 (about 20 minutes in all; Ben's slides are a share of it). The audience is statewide and non-technical:
ocean observing practitioners, agency staff and funders. Mark asked for 5 to 8 Explorer click-through slides, no
technical detail, and an arc that excites. Erin Satterthwaite will paste the slides into her Google deck (due
2026-10-07), so every slide must also work as a flat image. The deck was generated by a script. Its content, numbers,
order and speaker notes are right and must be kept; its design is functional but flat. Make it calm to sit through and
quick to read, using the CalCOFI brand kit and nothing else.

**The story arc (the polish should strengthen it, not replace it)**

77 years of one ocean, scattered across files, becomes one record per dataset; every audience then has a door to it; the
same water can be seen four ways (a map, a hexagon pattern, a surface, a slice); and in two weeks a research cruise
leaves to watch an El Niño, which we want people to see as it happens. Each slide says one thing, in one sentence Mark can
say without notes. Where a slide's design can carry that sentence (a bigger number, a clearer arrow, a cleaner
hierarchy), do that. Do not add slides, copy, claims or numbers.

**Hard rules**

1. **Keep the content exactly.** Keep all 21 slides, their order and titles, every number, every URL and every label as
   they are. Keep the speaker notes word for word, including the lines that begin `RESHOOT`. Do not round or restate a
   number.
2. **The four build-step slides stay four slides, in order** (slides 5, 6, 7, 8: who you are, then your door, then one
   database, then the portals). They are one picture drawn four times, each adding one column, so it animates the same
   way in Google Slides, a PDF or a projector without an animation engine.
   - Keep every shape in the same position on all four, so flipping between them shows only the new column appearing.
   - The yellow bar under a column header marks the column that just appeared. Keep exactly one per slide.
   - On slide 8, the CalOOS tile stays the highlighted one, and the line "CalOOS links to the authoritative dataset. Nothing
     is copied." stays. That is Erin's message and it must stay intact on every slide that touches CalOOS (slides 3, 8).
   - Do not merge the four into one slide with animations. Do not paste a screenshot of the diagram; keep it native.
3. **Brand only.** Use only the attached `brand/v2/` kit; `theme.css` holds the tokens.
   - **Palette:** UCSD Navy `#182B49`, Blue `#00629B`, Yellow `#FFCD00` (at most one accent per slide, with navy text on

4. **Mood by slide type.** The title slide (1) and the closing slide (21) may be dark (navy). The content slides stay

5. **Screenshots are evidence.** They are real captures of the live apps (the Explorer and CTD Transects) and must stay

   - Enlarge them where space allows; never crop away the header with its release stamp, the Controls panel or the

   - Slides 19 (before and after) and 20 (two placeholder frames) keep their labels: "BEFORE", "AFTER", "PLACEHOLDER
     SHOT". The placeholders will be replaced by real shots after the Explorer and CTD Transects work lands; do not fill

6. **Plain English.** The audience is not technical. Do not introduce a technical word the slide does not already use.
7. **Logo.** The horizontal lockup sits top-right on light slides and top-left on dark ones.
8. **Images.** No stock imagery, no clip art, no gradients beyond the brand's.
9. **Every slide must survive being a flat PNG.** No text smaller than 11 pt, nothing positioned off the slide, nothing that
   depends on an animation or a hover.

**Return**
