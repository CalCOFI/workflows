## The prompt

You are polishing a 24-slide, 16:9 PowerPoint deck for a 15-minute talk to the CalCOFI data team on
2026-09-08 ("CalCOFI.io — what landed since Aug 25, and what we need from each other"). The deck was
generated by a script, so its content, numbers, speaker notes and order are right and must be kept; its
design is functional but flat. Make it soothing to sit through and exciting to look at, using the CalCOFI
brand kit — nothing else — and keep every slide honest to its source.

**Hard rules**

1. Keep all 24 slides, their order, their titles, every number and every URL exactly as they are. Numbers
   were read from the release record; do not round, restate or "improve" them. Keep the speaker notes
   word for word.
2. Use only the brand kit in the attached `brand/v2/` folder (the README is the contract; `theme.css`
   holds the tokens). Palette: UCSD Navy `#182B49`, UCSD Blue `#00629B` (links, active), Yellow
   `#FFCD00` (one call-to-action accent per slide, navy text on it), Sand `#F5F0E6` (the alternating
   band), Gold `#C69214`, Cyan `#00C6D7` (sparingly), Cool Gray `#747678`, Stone `#B6B1A9`. Dark ground
   `#0F1A2E` with navy `#182B49` panels. Fonts: Source Sans 3 for everything, Teko (uppercase) for
   display headings only; both are in `brand/v2/fonts/` (OFL). No other colours, fonts or stock imagery.
3. Two moods, both on brand: light slides (white ground, navy type, sand bands) for content; navy dark
   slides for the opener, the section dividers and the closer, exactly as the specimen page
   (`assets/brand/brand_v2_light.png` / `_dark.png`) shows the two themes.
4. Screenshots are evidence, not decoration: never crop away a UI element that a bullet refers to, never
   recolour or restyle them, keep them large enough to read at the back of a room. Frame them with the
   brand's rounded borderless card (8 px corners, soft shadow) on a sand or navy band.
5. The horizontal CalCOFI lockup (`logo_calcofi_h.svg` on dark, `logo_calcofi_h_light.svg` on light) sits
   far left in a slim header on every content slide; the footer keeps "calcofi.io · CalCOFI data meeting ·
   2026-09-08 · N". Do not invent a UCSD or SIO logo; CalCOFI is a three-way partnership (NOAA, CDFW, SIO)
   and partner marks appear only if you have the actual files.
6. Accessibility: every text/background pair at 4.5:1 or better (the tokens already are); minimum 18 pt
   body, 14 pt captions; nothing important carried by colour alone.

**Playful motifs — the theme is "from ship to screen"**

The landing page's hero (slide 3) is a ship on Line 90 with the instruments hanging into the water
column over a real GEBCO sea-floor profile. Reuse that idea as a recurring, restrained motif:

- A thin sea-floor silhouette (from the hero) as a footer or band edge on section dividers, never behind
  text.
- The brand's own icon set (`brand/v2/icons/`, 51 glyphs: the six lens icons — stations, hexagons,
  contours, cruises, regions, sections; the category glyphs — phytoplankton, zooplankton, krill,
  ichthyoplankton, fish, whale, bottle, CTD, etc.) as small navy or blue marks beside headings, as the
  "numbers" of numbered lists, and as a "cast of characters" strip on the divider slides (from
  diatoms to whales, small to large, the way the landing page's Life tile does it).
- Depth as a vertical axis: where a slide lists steps (Ingest → Integrate → Publish → Visualize →
  Synthesize on the docs slide; Select → Refine → Share on the Explorer anatomy slide), let the sequence
  descend like a CTD cast, with a faint depth ruler, rather than left-to-right chevrons.
- Subtle, consistent entrance animations only (fade or wipe, 0.3 s, one per build); no bouncing, no
  sounds, no transitions between slides other than a plain fade.

**Per-slide guidance**

- 1 (title, dark): the four fact chips (release, datasets, rows, DOI) should read like the landing page's
  numbers band (Teko numerals, small caps labels). Add the ship-to-screen silhouette low on the slide.
- 2 (map of the talk): five cards as a horizontal "stations" line with a lens icon or category glyph on
  each; keep the one-sentence footer.
- 3 (front door hero) and 4 (the rest of the front door): let the screenshots breathe; on 4 the bento
  screenshot is tall, so give it the left two-thirds and the bullets the right third.
- 5 (brand): this slide is the brand talking about itself — make it the most beautifully composed one.
  Specimen light and dark side by side at the top, lockup / buttons / chips / icon sheet below as a tidy
  grid with tiny labels. Keep the line that all of it is tweakable.
- 6–10 (Explorer): one visual language across the five slides. On 7 the numbered yellow callouts on the
  screenshot must stay aligned with the legend; on 8 the three columns (layers, pane controls, help) need
  equal weight; on 9 the six lens tiles should each be headed by the lens icon and name in navy, with the
  thumbnail below and one line of type; 10 is the hero image of the talk — full-bleed screenshot with the
  fit line as a caption chip.
- 11 (hosting): the two response-time sparklines are the punchline (147 ms vs 11,353 ms); make the
  contrast visible with size and a single blue vs gold pairing, no red. The cost bullet is read from
  the console figures; keep it exactly.
- 12 (attribution): four small screenshots in a row (Explorer sources modal, Share → Cite, a dataset
  page's Cite section, the docs cite table) then the "what still needs filling" table — make the table
  scan: the Missing column as a navy numeral, the dataset keys as quiet chips.
- 13 (feedback): the annotated-screenshot dialog is the hero on the left; the three header bars on the
  right must line up so the identical speech-bubble button reads as one column; "Register a product"
  gets a sand callout.
- 14–20: tidy the tables (flextable output) into brand tables — navy header row, sand zebra rows, no
  vertical rules; keep every cell's text.
- 21 (docs): the "five doors" table and the five-part sidebar are the story; the DMP phases could descend
  like a cast (see motif). 22 (asks): this is the working slide of the meeting, keep it plain and legible;
  bold the owner column. 23 (closer, dark) and 24 (URL appendix): keep as is, restyled.
